      TACTICAL COMBAT & STEALTH GUIDE

      • Hunter Vision Cones: Enemy guards, heavy troopers, drones, and cameras project real-time floor detection cones and radar vectors. Stay outside cones or behind solid cover walls.
      • Acoustic Stealth: Crouching (Ctrl or C) silences footsteps. Firing the suppressed Spectre-9 keeps acoustic signatures minimal (5m), whereas unsuppressed rifles alert enemies up to 28m away.
      • Electronic Warfare: Use the Arc Caster (5) or your Phantom Cloak + EMP (Q) to temporarily disable Scout Drones and Surveillance Cameras, or hack the sector Security Terminal (E) to permanently shut down cameras.
      • Headshot Precision: Aim at enemy heads (especially while aiming down sights with RMB) for 2.0x–2.5x critical damage multipliers.
